# Remote Work Guidelines

## Eligibility

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with the approval of
their line manager. New joiners work on site for their first 30 days.

## Core hours

Remote employees must be reachable during core hours, 10:00 to 16:00 local
time.

## Equipment

The company provides a laptop and a one-time home-office allowance of 400
dollars. Equipment remains company property and is returned on departure.

## Working from abroad

Working from another country is limited to 20 working days per year and needs
written approval from HR at least 4 weeks in advance.
