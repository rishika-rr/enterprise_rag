# Expense Policy

## Submitting claims

Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date, with
itemised receipts attached. Claims submitted later than 30 days need approval
from the finance director.

## Meals and travel

The meal allowance while travelling is 60 dollars per day. Economy class is
booked for flights shorter than 6 hours; premium economy is allowed for longer
flights.

## Approval limits

Line managers approve claims up to 1,000 dollars. Claims above 1,000 dollars
also require approval from the department head.

## Reimbursement

Approved claims are reimbursed with the next monthly payroll run.
